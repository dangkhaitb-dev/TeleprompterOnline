About SolPrompter

The Organization Behind SolPrompter

SolPrompter builds and publishes a browser-based teleprompter for people who want to prepare a message, speak clearly on camera, and record without complicated software.

Open SolPrompter ↗

Why We Built SolPrompter

A Teleprompter Should Support the Speaker

Speaking to a camera is already demanding. A presenter is thinking about the message, the lens, the pace, and the recording while trying to sound like themselves.

We believe the teleprompter should adapt to the speaker, not ask the speaker to adapt to the tool.

That is why SolPrompter brings voice-following scroll, a camera-adjacent reading mode, remote control, scripts, and recording into one browser workspace.

SolPrompter also publishes practical guides on scripts, meetings, interviews, presentations, and speaking on camera. The product and the editorial work share one aim: make prepared communication easier to deliver naturally.

Our Mission

Make Professional Presenting Accessible

We build a practical teleprompter for people who need to communicate well, from a first recording on a phone to repeatable work for a team or studio.

  1. 01

    Natural Delivery

    Help presenters control their pace and wording without sounding rushed, robotic, or over-rehearsed.

  2. 02

    Better Eye Contact

    Keep the script near the camera so the viewer receives the attention that prepared delivery is meant to create.

  3. 03

    Practical Technology

    Turn useful teleprompter controls into a clear browser experience that works across common devices.

  4. 04

    Open Access

    Keep a capable presenting and recording workflow available without ads or a branded watermark on the finished video.

What Makes SolPrompter Different

A Connected Speaking Workflow

SolPrompter is designed around the work before, during, and after a take, rather than a single scrolling control.

  1. 01

    AI Voice Scroll

    The script follows your speech instead of forcing you to match a fixed scrolling speed.

  2. 02

    Notch Teleprompter Mode

    A compact reading area can be positioned close to the camera to reduce visible eye movement.

  3. 03

    Remote Control

    Control scrolling and navigate your script from another phone, tablet or computer.

  4. 04

    Professional Recording

    Present and record in one workflow with support for Full HD and 4K video on compatible devices.

  5. 05

    Script and Recording Management

    Create, organize and reuse scripts while keeping recordings connected to your content.

  6. 06

    Designed for Every Screen

    Use SolPrompter from a desktop, laptop, tablet or smartphone through a responsive, touch-friendly interface.

Our Product Principles

How We Make Product Decisions

  1. 01

    Simple by Default

    The essential action should be clear before a person has read a tutorial or opened a long settings panel.

  2. 02

    Capable When Needed

    More advanced controls should be available when they solve a real delivery or recording problem.

  3. 03

    Natural Delivery First

    Technology should support a presenter’s rhythm, eye line, and attention instead of making delivery feel mechanical.

  4. 04

    Useful Across Devices

    A phone, tablet, laptop, and desktop each need a considered workflow, not the same interface squeezed into a smaller screen.

  5. 05

    Focused Workspaces

    The interface should direct attention to the script, camera, and audience instead of advertisements or decorative noise.

  6. 06

    Respect for Your Work

    Scripts and recordings are the presenter’s work. SolPrompter is built without a branded watermark over exported videos.

Developing the Presentation Workspace

Our Current Commitment

What We Offer Today

SolPrompter is currently free to use. It is built without distracting advertisements or a branded watermark on exported video.

We publish product updates and practical guides so people can understand how the tool works, decide whether it fits their workflow, and prepare a better delivery.

As the product develops, we will keep the same standard: a clear, reliable experience that earns a place in a presenter’s work.

The Future of SolPrompter

Developing the Presentation Workspace

The work extends beyond basic teleprompter scrolling.

We are developing SolPrompter as one workspace for preparing a script, presenting it naturally, controlling a session remotely, recording a take, and managing the resulting work.

Future work will focus on useful automation, collaboration, and cross-device workflows that reduce the gap between writing an idea and recording it clearly.

A feature belongs in SolPrompter when it solves a real presentation problem and makes the workflow easier to trust.
