About SolPrompter

Present Naturally. Stay Focused.

SolPrompter is an online AI teleprompter built to help people speak confidently, maintain natural eye contact and create professional videos without complicated software.

Why We Built SolPrompter

Teleprompters Should Adapt to You

Most teleprompter tools expect presenters to follow a fixed scrolling speed, constantly adjust controls or combine several different applications to write, present and record.

We believe the teleprompter should adapt to the speaker—not the other way around.

SolPrompter was created to make presenting feel more natural. AI Voice Scroll follows your speaking pace, Notch Mode keeps your script close to the camera and remote control lets you manage the presentation without interrupting your recording.

Everything is designed to help you focus less on the software and more on the message you want to deliver.

Our Mission

Make Professional Presenting Accessible

Our mission is to build a powerful, easy-to-use teleprompter that works for everyone—from first-time creators recording on a phone to professional teams managing studio productions.

  1. 01

    Natural Delivery

    Help presenters speak at their own pace without sounding rushed, robotic or overly rehearsed.

  2. 02

    Better Eye Contact

    Place scripts closer to the camera so presentations feel more direct, confident and human.

  3. 03

    Simple Technology

    Turn advanced teleprompter features into an interface that feels clear and comfortable on every device.

  4. 04

    Accessible Creation

    Make professional presenting and recording tools available without unnecessary barriers, distracting advertising or branded watermarks.

What Makes SolPrompter Different

More Than a Scrolling Script

SolPrompter brings the complete presentation workflow into one connected workspace.

  1. 01

    AI Voice Scroll

    The script follows your speech instead of forcing you to match a fixed scrolling speed.

  2. 02

    Notch Teleprompter Mode

    A compact reading area can be positioned close to the camera to reduce visible eye movement.

  3. 03

    Remote Control

    Control scrolling and navigate your script from another phone, tablet or computer.

  4. 04

    Professional Recording

    Present and record in one workflow with support for Full HD and 4K video on compatible devices.

  5. 05

    Script and Recording Management

    Create, organize and reuse scripts while keeping recordings connected to your content.

  6. 06

    Designed for Every Screen

    Use SolPrompter from a desktop, laptop, tablet or smartphone through a responsive, touch-friendly interface.

Our Product Principles

Designed Around the Presenter

  1. 01

    Simple by Default

    Essential actions should be immediately understandable without tutorials, complex settings or a long setup process.

  2. 02

    Powerful When Needed

    Advanced controls should remain available without making the everyday experience feel complicated.

  3. 03

    Natural Over Robotic

    Technology should support the presenter’s speaking rhythm, eye movement and delivery rather than making the presentation feel mechanical.

  4. 04

    Useful on Every Device

    Mobile users should receive a complete and thoughtful experience—not a reduced desktop interface squeezed onto a smaller screen.

  5. 05

    Focused and Distraction-Free

    The interface should keep attention on the script, camera and audience instead of unnecessary visual elements.

  6. 06

    Respect for Your Content

    Your scripts and recordings belong to you. SolPrompter is designed to help you create without placing a branded watermark over your work.
