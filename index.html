FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I avoid looking like I am reading and maintain eye contact?

Activate SolPrompter's Notch Mode to position your script directly below the lens. This narrows the text width to 50-70 characters and anchors it at the top-center. By reducing eye movement to under 3 degrees, it gives the appearance that you are speaking directly to the viewer.

Why choose SolPrompter over an App Store application?

SolPrompter works directly on the web, requires no download, and does not put watermarks on your video exports. Most mobile applications limit script length or charge monthly fees to toggle simple features. SolPrompter provides full functionality out of the box.

Will the remote disconnect when my phone locks or goes to sleep?

No, SolPrompter's remote uses persistent WebSockets with a heartbeat protocol to ensure stability. As long as the teleprompter tab remains active, the link stays alive with under 10ms latency, preventing the dropouts common in hardware clickers.

Can I use SolPrompter with a physical glass beamsplitter rig?

Yes, toggle "Mirror Mode" in SolPrompter's settings to flip the text horizontally. To maximize reading contrast, switch the interface to dark mode (white text on a pure black background) to eliminate double reflections.

Are my scripts and corporate drafts secure?

Yes. Your scripts and recordings remain on your device while you work. SolPrompter does not upload this content to its servers, helping keep confidential drafts under your control.

Why does the reflected text on my glass look blurry?

Double-reflections (ghosting) happen on normal glass. Use professional beamsplitter glass and lower screen brightness. A professional 70/30 glass has an anti-reflective coating on one side. Draping the back of the glass with a dark cloth is also necessary to prevent light leaks.

Can I run the teleprompter offline?

The teleprompter workspace operates offline once loaded, but voice sync requires internet. The speech recognition uses native browser APIs, which process audio via Google or Apple cloud servers. For offline settings, switch to fixed-speed or manual scrolling.

How do I determine the right font size for my reading distance?

A general benchmark is to size your font to 1 inch of height for every 10 feet of reading distance. For close-up laptop reading, a smaller, narrow text layout is preferred to prevent eyes from visibly scanning left and right.
