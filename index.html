Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I avoid the stiff "hostage reader" look and maintain natural eye contact while reading?

You can maintain a completely natural gaze by activating SolPrompter's Notch Mode to keep the script directly beneath your camera lens. This feature shrinks the reading area width (keeping lines to 50-70 characters) and anchors it at the absolute top center of the viewport. This reduces eye travel (horizontal and vertical deviation) to under 3 degrees, ensuring your audience sees you looking directly into the camera lens, not scanning a screen.

Why should I choose SolPrompter over a dedicated App Store teleprompter app?

SolPrompter is a completely web-native workspace that runs instantly without downloads, subscriptions, watermark exports, or annoying ad banner interruptions. Most apps on iOS/Android lock script management, custom scroll speed, and mirror mode behind paywalls. SolPrompter provides all these premium features on a clean, modern web interface with zero registration required.

Will the remote control disconnect if my phone screen locks or goes to sleep?

No, SolPrompter's remote control system utilizes persistent WebSockets with an automatic heartbeat protocol to ensure connection stability even when idle. As long as the browser tab is active, the link between the tablet and the control phone (paired in 3 seconds via QR code) remains connected with a latency of under 10ms. This prevents the frustrating timeouts common with hardware clickers.

How do I setup a physical glass beamsplitter rig with this web application?

You can toggle "Mirror Mode" in SolPrompter's display settings to instantly flip the script text horizontally. Physical teleprompters reflect your screen image onto a 45-degree beamsplitter glass, which reverses the image naturally. For optimal clarity, set the interface to Dark Mode (white text on a pure black background) to eliminate double-image "ghosting" reflections in the glass.

Is it safe to paste confidential business scripts or unreleased product drafts?

Yes, SolPrompter is completely secure because all scripts and recordings are stored locally inside your browser's private memory (LocalStorage/IndexedDB). Because no script data is transmitted to SolPrompter's servers, your confidential business reviews, unreleased video drafts, or notes remain 100% private and protected on your own device.

Why does my text on the teleprompter glass look blurry or double-imaged?

This double-imaging (ghosting) is caused by light reflecting off both the front and back of standard glass; you can resolve this by using a professional beamsplitter glass and lowering screen brightness. Standard window glass has two reflective surfaces. A professional 70/30 teleprompter beamsplitter glass has an anti-reflective coating on one side to eliminate double reflections. Shrouding the back of the glass with a dark cloth is also essential to prevent glare.

Can I use this online teleprompter without an active internet connection?

The basic teleprompter workspace operates offline once the web page is fully loaded, but the AI Voice Scroll feature requires an active internet connection. SolPrompter's voice activation uses the browser's native Web Speech API, which transmits voice queries to Google or Apple cloud servers for speech-to-text processing. If your shoot has no internet connection, you can easily fall back to SolPrompter's manual scrolling or adjustable-speed auto-scroll.

How do I determine the correct font size and distance for reading comfort?

The recommended benchmark is to set your font height to 1 inch for every 10 feet of distance between you and the teleprompter screen. For close-up desktop recordings (within 3-4 feet), a smaller, condensed reading area is preferred because a wide layout forces your eyes to scan visibly from left to right, breaking camera connection.
