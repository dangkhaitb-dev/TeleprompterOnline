Problem: Traditional teleprompters scroll at a fixed speed

If you speak faster, you lose your place. If you pause, the script keeps moving. This makes presentations feel rigid and stressful.

Solution: AI Voice-Activated Scrolling

SolPrompter listens and advances the script as you read, adjusting to changes in your speaking pace.

Voice recognition follows your delivery and advances the script as you speak, responding whenever your pace changes.

  • Stay focused

  • Minimize retakes

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I avoid looking like I am reading and maintain eye contact?

Activate SolPrompter's Notch Mode to position your script directly below the lens. A narrower reading column reduces visible side-to-side scanning, but the result still depends on your screen size, camera position, and reading distance.

Why choose SolPrompter over an App Store application?

SolPrompter works directly on the web, requires no download, and does not put watermarks on your video exports. It is a practical option when you want to paste a script and start without installing or creating an account.

Will the remote disconnect when my phone locks or goes to sleep?

SolPrompter's remote uses a persistent WebSocket session with a heartbeat to detect connection loss. Actual latency and behavior while a phone is locked depend on the devices, browser power-saving rules, and network quality.

Can I use SolPrompter with a physical glass beamsplitter rig?

Yes, toggle "Mirror Mode" in SolPrompter's settings to flip the text horizontally. To maximize reading contrast, switch the interface to dark mode (white text on a pure black background) to eliminate double reflections.

Are my scripts and corporate drafts secure?

Your scripts and recordings remain on your device while you work. SolPrompter does not upload that content to its servers. Voice recognition may still use an on-device engine or a recognition service supplied by your browser.

Why does the reflected text on my glass look blurry?

Double-reflections (ghosting) happen on normal glass. Use professional beamsplitter glass and lower screen brightness. A professional 70/30 glass has an anti-reflective coating on one side. Draping the back of the glass with a dark cloth is also necessary to prevent light leaks.

Can I run the teleprompter offline?

The teleprompter workspace can continue after the page has loaded, but voice sync availability depends on your browser's recognition mode. Use fixed-speed or manual scrolling when an offline speech-recognition engine is unavailable.

How do I determine the right font size for my reading distance?

Increase the font size as your reading distance grows, then test it at the exact camera position before recording. For close laptop reading, use a narrower text column to reduce visible side-to-side scanning.
