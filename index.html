Natural Eye Contact

Keep Your Script Close to the Camera

VTeleprompter’s notch mode places a compact reading area near your camera, helping your eyes stay focused closer to the lens.

  • Reduce obvious side-to-side eye movement.
  • Place the script near your webcam or phone camera.
  • Resize the reading area to show only the lines you need.
  • Move the teleprompter anywhere on your screen.
  • Maintain more natural eye contact during recordings and video calls.

Notch mode is ideal for interviews, presentations, product demos, online lessons, video calls and talking-head content.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is VTeleprompter free to use?

VTeleprompter is currently free to use. You can access its essential teleprompter, script and recording features without advertisements or a branded video watermark.

What is AI Voice Scroll?

AI Voice Scroll listens to your speech and moves through the script based on your natural speaking pace. It helps you pause, continue and present without constantly adjusting a fixed scroll speed.

What is VTeleprompter Notch Mode?

Notch Mode places a compact teleprompter reading area close to your camera. This helps reduce visible eye movement and makes your eye contact appear more natural during recordings, presentations and video calls.

Can I use VTeleprompter on a phone or tablet?

Yes. VTeleprompter has a responsive interface designed for smartphones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers. Available features may depend on the browser, camera and device capabilities.

Do I need to install VTeleprompter?

No installation is required for the web version. You can open VTeleprompter in a supported modern browser, add your script and start presenting online.

Can I control VTeleprompter from another device?

Yes. VTeleprompter remote control lets you use another phone, tablet or computer to start or pause the teleprompter, adjust scrolling and navigate through your script.

Can VTeleprompter record videos in 4K?

VTeleprompter supports Full HD and 4K recording when the camera, browser and device support the selected resolution. Actual recording quality depends on the capabilities and permissions of your device.

Does VTeleprompter add a watermark to recordings?

VTeleprompter does not add a branded watermark to your recorded videos.

Which script file formats does VTeleprompter support?

VTeleprompter supports pasting text directly and uploading supported script documents such as TXT, DOCX, PDF and Markdown files.

Can VTeleprompter be used as an online autocue?

Yes. VTeleprompter can be used as a browser-based online autocue with manual scrolling, fixed-speed automatic scrolling and AI voice-activated scrolling.
