Billing

Questions About Payment

What are the AI Scroll rate limits?

AI Scroll limits are sized for normal individual creator usage. To ensure reliable service for everyone, extremely excessive, automated or abusive usage may be limited under our Fair Use Policy.

Will these prices go up?

Yes. These are early adopter prices and they will increase as we add more premium features. While your subscription stays active without interruption, you keep the price you signed up at; a Lifetime purchase is a one-time payment that is never affected by later price changes.

What does the Lifetime plan actually cover?

A single payment for the Pro features, plus every update and premium feature we release for SolPrompter, with no renewal. Lifetime refers to the lifetime of the product, not the lifetime of the purchaser — it lasts for as long as SolPrompter is commercially operated by us. The full terms are in the Billing and Refund Policy.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Cancel at any time from your account billing settings, or email contact.solplatform@gmail.com. Automatic renewal stops immediately and you keep paid access until the end of the billing period you already paid for.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a voluntary 7-day refund guarantee on your first purchase made directly through SolPrompter, subject to the conditions in the Billing and Refund Policy. Mandatory consumer rights in your country always apply on top of it.

What happens when the One-Day Pass expires?

Pro features simply stop after 24 hours and the pass does not renew. Your scripts and recordings stay where they are, and you can buy another pass or move to Pro whenever you want.

Do the prices include tax?

Unless stated otherwise, displayed prices exclude taxes that must be collected separately. The final amount, currency and any applicable VAT or sales tax are shown before you confirm the payment.
